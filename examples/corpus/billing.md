# Billing

Northwind Cloud bills every workspace monthly, in arrears. The billing period always
starts on the day the workspace was created.

## Invoices

Invoices are generated on the first day of each month and emailed to every user with
the Billing Admin role. A PDF copy stays available under Settings, Billing, History
for seven years.

## Refunds

Refunds are returned to the original payment card within 5 business days. Card issuers
may take longer to show the credit on a statement. Refunds cannot be issued to a
different card than the one used for the original charge.

## Currency and tax

Workspaces are billed in the currency chosen at signup and the currency cannot be
changed afterwards. VAT is added for workspaces with a billing address inside the EU
unless a valid VAT number is on file.

## Plan changes

Upgrading a plan takes effect immediately and is prorated for the rest of the billing
period. Downgrading takes effect at the start of the next billing period, so the seats
stay available until then.
