# Operations

## Regions

Workspaces run in one of three regions: eu-central, us-east and ap-south. The region is
chosen at signup and data never leaves it. Moving a workspace between regions requires
a support request and a scheduled maintenance window.

## Backups

Database backups run daily at 02:00 UTC and are retained for 30 days. Restoring a
backup is done by support and takes up to four hours, during which the workspace is
read only.

## Status and incidents

Incidents are published on the status page within 15 minutes of detection. Subscribers
receive an email for every state change until the incident is marked resolved.
