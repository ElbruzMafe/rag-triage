# Accounts and access

## Password reset

Users reset their own password from the sign-in screen. The reset link is valid for
30 minutes and can only be used once. An administrator cannot set a password on behalf
of a user.

## Two-factor authentication

Two-factor authentication uses time-based one-time codes. Workspace owners can make it
mandatory for every member, and members who have not enrolled are asked to do so on
their next sign-in.

## Seats

A seat is consumed by any member who has signed in at least once during the billing
period. Invited members who never sign in do not consume a seat. Removing a member
frees the seat at the start of the next billing period.

## Roles

There are four roles: Owner, Billing Admin, Member and Read Only. Only an Owner can
delete a workspace, and a workspace must always keep at least one Owner.
